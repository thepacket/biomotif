# The motif library

487 motifs in 57 categories. Generated by `python tools/make_index.py`; edit the `.mtf` files, not this page.

A motif marked *template* matches almost anywhere and is skipped by `scan` unless you pass `:all #t`. Search for it by name instead.

## Bacterial and archaeal elements

Defined in `motif/lib/prokaryote.mtf`.

### attenuation

**`attenuator-trp-leader`** *(template)*

The tandem tryptophan codons in the trpL leader peptide. Slow translation here when
tryptophan is scarce lets the antiterminator form.

- pattern: `(seq "TGG" "TGG")`
- example: `TGGTGG`

### coding

**`start-codon`** *(template)*

Bacterial start codon. ATG is used by roughly 80% of E. coli genes, GTG by 12% and TTG by
8%.

- pattern: `(alt "ATG" "GTG" "TTG")`
- example: `ATG`

**`stop-codon`** *(template)*

Any of the three stop codons. TAA is the most common in E. coli.

- pattern: `(alt "TAA" "TAG" "TGA")`
- example: `TAA`

### crispr

**`crispr-repeat-ecoli`**

Direct repeat of the E. coli type I-E CRISPR array. Spacers of about 32 bases sit between
copies.

- pattern: `(fuzzy 2 "GTGTTCCCCGCGCCAGCGGGGATAAACCG")`

### nucleoid

**`fis-site`** *(template)*

Fis binding site. Fis is the most abundant nucleoid protein in fast growth and activates
rRNA promoters.

- pattern: `(iupac "GNTYAAAWTTTRANC")`

**`ihf-site`** *(template)*

Integration host factor binding site. IHF bends DNA by up to 160 degrees and organises the
nucleoid as well as phage lambda integration.

- pattern: `(iupac "WATCAANNNNTTR")`
- example: `TATCAAGCATTTA`

### operator

**`ara-i1`**

araI1 half-site of the araBAD operator, bound by AraC.

- pattern: `(fuzzy 2 "AGCGGATCCTACCTGACGCT")`

**`crp-site`**

CRP (CAP) binding site. The core is a TGTGA half-site, six bases, then the inverted TCACA.
Binding bends the DNA by about 90 degrees.

- pattern: `(seq (named 'left "TGTGA") (gap 6 6) (named 'right "TCACA"))`
- example: `TGTGAtctagaTCACA`
- reference: Berg & von Hippel 1988, J Mol Biol 200:709

**`fur-box`**

Fur box, the iron-responsive operator bound by Fur loaded with Fe(II).

- pattern: `(fuzzy 3 "GATAATGATAATCATTATC")`

**`lac-operator`**

The primary lac operator O1, a near-palindrome bound by the Lac repressor. Two auxiliary
operators O2 and O3 loop the DNA with it.

- pattern: `(fuzzy 2 "AATTGTGAGCGGATAACAATT")`
- example: `AATTGTGAGCGGATAACAATT`
- reference: Gilbert & Maxam 1973, PNAS 70:3581

**`lac-operator-symmetric`**

The perfectly palindromic lac operator Osym, which binds LacI more tightly than the natural
O1 and is used in tightly repressed expression vectors.

- pattern: `"AATTGTGAGCGCTCACAATT"`

**`lexa-box`**

LexA box (SOS box). LexA represses the SOS regulon until RecA-stimulated autocleavage
removes it after DNA damage.

- pattern: `(seq "CTGT" (gap 8 8) "ACAG")`
- example: `CTGTatatatatACAG`
- reference: Wertman & Mount 1985, J Bacteriol 163:376

**`tet-operator`**

tetO2, bound by the Tet repressor and released by tetracycline or doxycycline. The basis of
Tet-On and Tet-Off systems.

- pattern: `"TCCCTATCAGTGATAGAGA"`

**`trp-operator`**

trp operator, overlapping the trp promoter and bound by TrpR charged with tryptophan.

- pattern: `(fuzzy 2 "ACTAGTTAACTAGTACGCAAGTT")`

### promoter

**`discriminator`** *(template)*

GC-rich discriminator between the -10 box and the start site. Its length and composition set
the promoter's sensitivity to ppGpp during the stringent response.

- pattern: `(seq (fuzzy 1 (iupac "TATAAT")) (run "GC" 4 9) (any-of "AG"))`

**`extended-minus-10`** *(template)*

Extended -10 promoter. A TG dinucleotide one base upstream of the -10 box can compensate for
a missing or weak -35 element.

- pattern: `(seq "TG" (gap 1 1) (fuzzy 1 (iupac "TATAAT")))`
- example: `TGnTATAAT`
- reference: Mitchell et al. 2003, Nucleic Acids Res 31:4689

**`minus-10`** *(template)*

Sigma-70 -10 element, the Pribnow box. Consensus TATAAT; positions T-12 and T-7 are the most
conserved and the least tolerant of substitution.

- pattern: `(iupac "TATAAT")`
- example: `TATAAT`
- reference: Pribnow 1975, PNAS 72:784

**`minus-35`** *(template)*

Sigma-70 -35 element. The consensus TTGACA is rarely matched exactly; most functional -35
boxes carry one or two mismatches.

- pattern: `(iupac "TTGACA")`
- example: `TTGACA`
- reference: Harley & Reynolds 1987, Nucleic Acids Res 15:2343

**`sigma28-promoter`**

Sigma-28 (FliA) flagellar promoter: -35 TAAA and -10 GCCGATAA.

- pattern: `(seq (iupac "TAAA") (gap 13 17) (iupac "GCCGATAA"))`

**`sigma32-minus-10`** *(template)*

Sigma-32 (RpoH) -10 element of the heat shock regulon.

- pattern: `(fuzzy 1 (iupac "CCCCATNTA"))`
- example: `CCCCATTTA`

**`sigma38-minus-10`** *(template)*

Sigma-38 (RpoS) -10 element of the general stress regulon. A C at -13 distinguishes many
RpoS-dependent promoters from sigma-70 ones.

- pattern: `(iupac "CTATACT")`
- example: `CTATACT`

**`sigma54-promoter`**

Sigma-54 (RpoN) promoter, recognised at -24/-12 rather than -35/-10. Needs an enhancer-bound
activator and ATP hydrolysis to open.

- pattern: `(seq (named 'box24 (iupac "TGGCAC")) (gap 5 5) (named 'box12 (iupac "TTGCW")))`
- example: `TGGCACgcgtaTTGCA`
- reference: Barrios et al. 1999, Nucleic Acids Res 27:4305

**`sigma70-promoter`**

A full sigma-70 promoter: -35 and -10 boxes separated by 15 to 19 bases, 17 being optimal.
Allows one mismatch in each box, which is what most real E. coli promoters look like.

- pattern: `(seq (named 'box35 (fuzzy 1 (iupac "TTGACA"))) (named 'spacer (gap 15 19)) (named 'box10 (fuzzy 1 (iupac "TATAAT"))))`
- example: `TTGACAgcgtacgtacgtacgTATAAT`
- reference: Hawley & McClure 1983, Nucleic Acids Res 11:2237

**`up-element`**

UP element: an AT-rich sequence upstream of -35 bound by the alpha subunit C-terminal
domain. Boosts transcription up to 30-fold at rrn promoters.

- pattern: `(iupac "AAAWWTWTTTTNNNAAANNN")`
- example: `AAAATTATTTTTGCAAAAGC`
- reference: Estrem et al. 1998, Genes Dev 12:2134

### rbs

**`ribosome-binding-site`**

A complete prokaryotic RBS: a Shine-Dalgarno core, a 4 to 12 base spacer and a start codon.
One mismatch is allowed in the SD, as most genes have.

- pattern: `(seq (named 'sd (fuzzy 1 (iupac "AGGAGG"))) (named 'spacer (gap 4 12)) (named 'start (alt "ATG" "GTG" "TTG")))`
- example: `AGGAGGtataccATG`
- reference: Ringquist et al. 1992, Mol Microbiol 6:1219

**`shine-dalgarno`** *(template)*

Shine-Dalgarno sequence, complementary to the 3' end of 16S rRNA (anti-SD CCUCC). The core
AGGAGG sits 5 to 10 bases upstream of the start codon.

- pattern: `(iupac "AGGAGG")`
- example: `AGGAGG`
- reference: Shine & Dalgarno 1974, PNAS 71:1342

**`strong-rbs`**

The strong synthetic RBS used in many expression vectors: a perfect Shine-Dalgarno with an
8-base spacer to ATG.

- pattern: `(seq "AAGGAGG" (gap 5 9) "ATG")`
- example: `AAGGAGGatatacatATG`

### recombination

**`chi-site`**

Chi site. RecBCD switches from destructive nuclease to recombinase when it runs into one,
which is why Chi sites are over-represented on the leading strand.

- pattern: `"GCTGGTGG"`
- example: `GCTGGTGG`
- reference: Smith et al. 1981, Cell 24:429

### repeat

**`rep-element`**

Repetitive extragenic palindromic (REP) element, a short palindrome present in hundreds of
copies in enterobacterial intergenic regions.

- pattern: `(fuzzy 2 "GCCGGATGCGGCGTGAACGCCTTATCCGGCCTAC")`

### replication

**`dnaa-box`** *(template)*

DnaA box. Five of them in oriC nucleate replication initiation.

- pattern: `(iupac "TTATNCACA")`
- example: `TTATCCACA`
- reference: Fuller et al. 1984, Cell 38:889

**`oric-13mer`** *(template)*

The AT-rich 13-mer repeat in the DNA unwinding element of oriC, where the duplex first
opens.

- pattern: `(iupac "GATCTNTTNTTTT")`
- example: `GATCTGTTCTTTT`

**`ter-site`** *(template)*

Ter site, bound by Tus to form a replication fork trap that permits forks to pass in one
direction only.

- pattern: `(iupac "GTTGTAACTAAAT")`

### riboswitch

**`riboswitch-tpp-core`** *(template)*

Conserved core of the thiamine pyrophosphate riboswitch aptamer, the most widespread
riboswitch class.

- pattern: `(seq (iupac "CTGAGA") (gap 20 60) (iupac "CTTGAACC"))`

### terminator

**`rho-independent-terminator`**

Intrinsic (Rho-independent) terminator: a GC-rich hairpin followed by a run of uridines. The
hairpin stalls RNA polymerase and the weak rU:dA hybrid lets the transcript fall off.

- pattern: `(seq (named 'hairpin (hairpin (stem 5 10) (loop 3 8))) (named 'utail (run "T" 4 9)))`
- example: `GCGCCcgatGGCGCTTTTTTT`
- reference: d'Aubenton Carafa et al. 1990, J Mol Biol 216:835

**`rrnb-t1-terminator`**

The rrnB T1 terminator, the standard strong terminator in E. coli vectors.

- pattern: `"AGCCCGCCTAATGAGCGGGCTTTTTTTT"`

**`t7-terminator`**

The T7 phage transcription terminator carried by pET vectors.

- pattern: `"CTAGCATAACCCCTTGGGGCCTCTAAACGGGTCTTGAGGGGTTTTTTG"`

### transposon

**`is1-inverted-repeat`**

Terminal inverted repeat of IS1, the smallest and most common E. coli insertion sequence.

- pattern: `(fuzzy 2 "GGTGATGCTGCCAACTTACT")`

**`tn5-mosaic-end`**

Tn5 mosaic end, the 19 bp sequence recognised by Tn5 transposase. Nextera and ATAC-seq use
it to insert sequencing adapters.

- pattern: `"CTGTCTCTTATACACATCT"`
- example: `CTGTCTCTTATACACATCT`


## Animal and fungal elements

Defined in `motif/lib/eukaryote.mtf`.

### core-promoter

**`bre-downstream`** *(template)*

TFIIB recognition element downstream of the TATA box.

- pattern: `(iupac "RTDKKKK")`

**`bre-upstream`** *(template)*

TFIIB recognition element upstream of the TATA box; sets promoter direction.

- pattern: `(iupac "SSRCGCC")`
- example: `GGACGCC`

**`caat-box`** *(template)*

CCAAT box, typically 60 to 100 bases upstream, bound by NF-Y or C/EBP.

- pattern: `"CCAAT"`
- example: `CCAAT`

**`dpe`** *(template)*

Downstream promoter element, 28 to 32 bases past the start site. Works with the Inr in TATA-
less promoters, especially in Drosophila.

- pattern: `(iupac "RGWYVT")`
- example: `AGACGT`
- reference: Burke & Kadonaga 1996, Genes Dev 10:711

**`gc-box`** *(template)*

GC box, the Sp1 binding site of housekeeping promoters. Often present in several copies in
CpG island promoters.

- pattern: `(iupac "KRGGCKRRK")`
- example: `GGGGCGGGG`

**`initiator`** *(template)*

Initiator element (Inr). The A is the transcription start site itself. Human consensus is
pyrimidine-pyrimidine-A-N-T/A-pyrimidine-pyrimidine.

- pattern: `(iupac "YYANWYY")`
- example: `TCAGTCT`
- reference: Smale & Baltimore 1989, Cell 57:103

**`mte`** *(template)*

Motif ten element, downstream of the start site; acts with the Inr independently of the TATA
box.

- pattern: `(iupac "CSARCSSAACGS")`

**`tata-box`**

TATA box, about 30 bases upstream of the start site in the third of human promoters that
have one. Bound by TBP, which bends the DNA by 80 degrees.

- pattern: `(iupac "TATAWAWR")`
- example: `TATAAAAG`
- reference: Bucher 1990, J Mol Biol 212:563

**`tata-box-strict`**

The narrow TATA consensus TATAAA, without the degenerate flanks.

- pattern: `"TATAAA"`
- example: `TATAAA`

**`tata-inr-promoter`**

A canonical TATA-plus-Inr core promoter: TATA box, 18 to 30 bases, then the initiator around
the start site.

- pattern: `(seq (named 'tata (iupac "TATAWAWR")) (named 'spacer (gap 18 30)) (named 'inr (iupac "YYANWYY")))`

**`tct-motif`** *(template)*

TCT motif, the polypyrimidine initiator of ribosomal protein genes, which replaces the
ordinary Inr in that class of promoters.

- pattern: `(iupac "YYCTTTYY")`

### insulator

**`ctcf-core`**

CTCF core motif. CTCF anchors chromatin loops and its orientation decides which loops form.

- pattern: `(iupac "CCRCNAGGNGGCRG")`
- reference: Kim et al. 2007, Cell 128:1231

### nuclear-receptor

**`direct-repeat-5`**

DR5: two AGGTCA half-sites in direct repeat with five spacer bases, the retinoic acid
receptor element.

- pattern: `(seq "AGGTCA" (gap 5 5) "AGGTCA")`

**`estrogen-response-element`**

Estrogen response element: two AGGTCA half-sites as an inverted repeat with a three-base
spacer.

- pattern: `(seq "GGTCA" (gap 3 3) "TGACC")`
- example: `GGTCAnnnTGACC`

**`glucocorticoid-response-element`**

Glucocorticoid response element, also used by the androgen, mineralocorticoid and
progesterone receptors.

- pattern: `(seq (iupac "GGWACA") (gap 3 3) (iupac "TGTWCY"))`
- example: `GGTACAnnnTGTTCT`

**`nuclear-receptor-half-site`** *(template)*

The AGGTCA half-site read by most nuclear receptors. Spacing and orientation of two half-
sites decide which receptor binds.

- pattern: `"AGGTCA"`
- example: `AGGTCA`

### polyadenylation

**`poly-a-signal`**

Canonical polyadenylation signal AATAAA, 10 to 30 bases upstream of the cleavage site. About
70% of human genes use it, another 15% use ATTAAA.

- pattern: `"AATAAA"`
- example: `AATAAA`
- reference: Proudfoot 2011, Genes Dev 25:1770

**`poly-a-signal-variant`**

The two main polyadenylation signals together, AATAAA and ATTAAA.

- pattern: `(alt "AATAAA" "ATTAAA")`

**`poly-a-site`**

A complete poly(A) site: the AATAAA hexamer, a spacer and the GU-rich downstream element
that CstF binds.

- pattern: `(seq (named 'hexamer (alt "AATAAA" "ATTAAA")) (named 'spacer (gap 10 30)) (named 'dse (iupac "YGTGTTYY")))`

### recombination

**`rss-12`** *(template)*

A 12-RSS: heptamer, 12-base spacer, nonamer. Recombines only with a 23-RSS, which is the
12/23 rule.

- pattern: `(seq (named 'heptamer "CACAGTG") (named 'spacer (gap 12 12)) (named 'nonamer (iupac "ACAAAAACC")))`

**`rss-23`** *(template)*

A 23-RSS: heptamer, 23-base spacer, nonamer.

- pattern: `(seq (named 'heptamer "CACAGTG") (named 'spacer (gap 23 23)) (named 'nonamer (iupac "ACAAAAACC")))`

**`rss-heptamer`** *(template)*

Recombination signal sequence heptamer, the RAG1/RAG2 cut site in V(D)J recombination.

- pattern: `"CACAGTG"`
- example: `CACAGTG`

### repeat

**`alu-box-a`** *(template)*

The A box of the internal RNA polymerase III promoter carried by Alu elements.

- pattern: `(iupac "TGGCTCACGCC")`

**`microsatellite-ca`** *(template)*

CA dinucleotide microsatellite, the classic polymorphic marker.

- pattern: `(repeat "CA" 8)`

**`trinucleotide-cag`** *(template)*

CAG trinucleotide repeat. Expansion past about 36 copies causes Huntington disease and
several spinocerebellar ataxias.

- pattern: `(repeat "CAG" 10)`
- reference: MacDonald et al. 1993, Cell 72:971

### replication

**`ars-consensus`** *(template)*

Autonomously replicating sequence consensus, the 11 bp core of a budding yeast replication
origin.

- pattern: `(iupac "WTTTAYRTTTW")`
- example: `ATTTATGTTTA`
- reference: Broach et al. 1983, Cold Spring Harb Symp 47:1165

### splicing

**`branch-point`** *(template)*

Intron branch point. The bulged A attacks the 5' splice site to form the lariat. Human
branch points are loose; yeast uses a rigid TACTAAC.

- pattern: `(iupac "YTNAY")`
- example: `CTGAC`

**`branch-point-yeast`** *(template)*

The invariant yeast branch point TACTAAC.

- pattern: `"TACTAAC"`
- example: `TACTAAC`

**`exonic-splicing-enhancer`** *(template)*

A purine-rich exonic splicing enhancer of the type bound by SR proteins.

- pattern: `(iupac "GARGAR")`
- example: `GAAGAA`

**`gc-ag-intron`** *(template)*

The minor GC-AG intron class, about 0.7% of human introns.

- pattern: `(seq "GC" (gap 20 2000) (run "CT" 5 25) (gap 0 5) "AG")`

**`gt-ag-intron`** *(template)*

A whole U2-type intron: GT at the donor, a branch point, a polypyrimidine tract and AG at
the acceptor. Over 99% of human introns are GT-AG.

- pattern: `(seq "GT" (gap 20 2000) (iupac "YTNAY") (run "CT" 5 25) (gap 0 5) "AG")`

**`polypyrimidine-tract`** *(template)*

Polypyrimidine tract upstream of the 3' splice site, bound by U2AF65. Its length and purity
predict splicing efficiency.

- pattern: `(run "CT" 10 25)`

**`splice-acceptor`**

3' splice site (acceptor): a polypyrimidine tract, then NCAG at the intron end. The AG is
nearly invariant.

- pattern: `(seq (named 'ppt (run "CT" 8 20)) (any-of "ACGT") (named 'ag "AG"))`
- example: `TTTTTTTTTTCAG`

**`splice-donor`**

5' splice site (donor). The GT at the start of the intron is nearly invariant; the flanking
bases pair with U1 snRNA.

- pattern: `(seq (named 'exon (iupac "MAG")) (named 'intron (iupac "GTRAGT")))`
- example: `CAGGTAAGT`
- reference: Shapiro & Senapathy 1987, Nucleic Acids Res 15:7155

**`u12-donor`**

U12-type (minor spliceosome) 5' splice site. These introns are rare but conserved and often
rate-limiting for their host gene.

- pattern: `(iupac "RTATCCTT")`

### structure

**`g-quadruplex`**

G-quadruplex forming sequence: four G-tracts separated by short loops. Common at telomeres,
promoters and replication origins.

- pattern: `(seq (run "G" 3 6) (gap 1 7) (run "G" 3 6) (gap 1 7) (run "G" 3 6) (gap 1 7) (run "G" 3 6))`
- example: `GGGTTAGGGTTAGGGTTAGGG`
- reference: Huppert & Balasubramanian 2005, Nucleic Acids Res 33:2908

**`z-dna-prone`** *(template)*

Alternating purine-pyrimidine tract that can flip to left-handed Z-DNA under negative
supercoiling.

- pattern: `(repeat (iupac "CG") 6)`

### telomere

**`telomere-repeat-plant`**

Plant telomere repeat TTTAGGG (Arabidopsis type).

- pattern: `"TTTAGGG"`

**`telomere-repeat-vertebrate`**

Vertebrate telomere repeat TTAGGG, added by telomerase.

- pattern: `"TTAGGG"`
- example: `TTAGGG`

**`telomere-repeat-worm`**

Caenorhabditis elegans telomere repeat TTAGGC.

- pattern: `"TTAGGC"`

**`telomere-tract`** *(template)*

A telomeric tract: at least three consecutive vertebrate repeats.

- pattern: `(repeat "TTAGGG" 3)`

### tfbs

**`antioxidant-response-element`** *(template)*

Antioxidant response element, bound by NRF2/sMAF under oxidative stress.

- pattern: `(iupac "TGACNNNGC")`

**`ap1-site`**

AP-1 site (TRE), bound by Fos/Jun heterodimers. One base shorter than the CRE.

- pattern: `"TGACTCA"`
- example: `TGACTCA`

**`carg-box`** *(template)*

CArG box, the serum response element bound by SRF.

- pattern: `(iupac "CCWWWWWWGG")`
- example: `CCATATTAGG`

**`cre`**

cAMP response element, bound by CREB as a dimer. A perfect palindrome.

- pattern: `"TGACGTCA"`
- example: `TGACGTCA`
- reference: Montminy et al. 1986, PNAS 83:6682

**`e-box`** *(template)*

E-box CANNTG, bound by basic helix-loop-helix factors. Which bHLH binds depends on the two
central bases.

- pattern: `(iupac "CANNTG")`
- example: `CACGTG`

**`e-box-myc`**

The CACGTG E-box preferred by Myc/Max and USF.

- pattern: `"CACGTG"`
- example: `CACGTG`

**`e-box-myod`** *(template)*

The CAGCTG E-box preferred by MyoD and other myogenic factors.

- pattern: `"CAGCTG"`

**`ets-site`** *(template)*

ETS family binding site built on the invariant GGA(A/T) core.

- pattern: `(iupac "MGGAAGT")`
- example: `AGGAAGT`

**`gal4-uas`** *(template)*

GAL4 upstream activating sequence: CGG triplets 11 bases apart, the binding site of the
yeast GAL4 zinc cluster.

- pattern: `(seq "CGG" (gap 11 11) "CCG")`
- example: `CGGnnnnnnnnnnnCCG`

**`gas-element`** *(template)*

Gamma-activated sequence, bound by STAT dimers after cytokine signalling.

- pattern: `(seq "TTC" (gap 3 3) "GAA")`
- example: `TTCnnnGAA`

**`gata-site`** *(template)*

GATA factor binding site. The GATA core is invariant; the flanking W and R set which family
member binds.

- pattern: `(iupac "WGATAR")`
- example: `AGATAA`

**`heat-shock-element`**

Heat shock element: inverted nGAAn repeats bound by HSF trimers. Three units make a
functional site.

- pattern: `(seq (iupac "NGAAN") (iupac "NTTCN") (iupac "NGAAN"))`
- example: `TGAACGTTCTAGAAC`
- reference: Amin et al. 1988, Mol Cell Biol 8:3761

**`hif-response-element`** *(template)*

Hypoxia response element. The RCGTG core is bound by HIF-1alpha/ARNT.

- pattern: `(iupac "RCGTG")`
- example: `ACGTG`

**`hnf1-site`**

HNF1 binding site, an inverted palindrome driving liver-specific genes.

- pattern: `(iupac "GTTAATNATTAAC")`

**`isre`**

Interferon-stimulated response element, bound by the ISGF3 complex.

- pattern: `(iupac "AGTTTCNNTTTC")`

**`mef2-site`** *(template)*

MEF2 binding site, an AT-rich relative of the CArG box.

- pattern: `(iupac "YTAWWWWTAR")`

**`nfkb-site`**

NF-kappaB binding site (kappaB element).

- pattern: `(iupac "GGGRNNYYCC")`
- example: `GGGACTTTCC`
- reference: Kunsch et al. 1992, Mol Cell Biol 12:4412

**`octamer`** *(template)*

Octamer motif ATGCAAAT, bound by POU factors Oct-1 and Oct-2.

- pattern: `"ATGCAAAT"`
- example: `ATGCAAAT`

**`p53-response-element`**

p53 response element: two decameric half-sites, separated by up to 13 bases.

- pattern: `(seq (named 'half1 (iupac "RRRCWWGYYY")) (gap 0 13) (named 'half2 (iupac "RRRCWWGYYY")))`
- example: `GGGCATGTCCGGGCAAGTCC`
- reference: el-Deiry et al. 1992, Nat Genet 1:45

**`rap1-site`** *(template)*

Rap1 binding site in yeast, found at telomeres, the silent mating loci and ribosomal protein
promoters.

- pattern: `(iupac "RMACCCANNCAYY")`

**`runx-site`** *(template)*

RUNX (CBF) binding site.

- pattern: `(iupac "TGYGGT")`

**`sp1-site`**

Sp1 binding site, the GC box in its full form.

- pattern: `(iupac "GGGGCGGGGC")`

### translation

**`kozak`**

Kozak sequence. The purine at -3 and the G at +4 matter most; a strong Kozak has both, an
adequate one has just the -3 purine.

- pattern: `(iupac "GCCRCCATGG")`
- example: `GCCACCATGG`
- reference: Kozak 1987, Nucleic Acids Res 15:8125

**`kozak-core`** *(template)*

The minimal Kozak context: a purine three bases before ATG and a G after it.

- pattern: `(seq (any-of "AG") (gap 2 2) "ATG" "G")`
- example: `ACCATGG`


## Plant cis-regulatory elements

Defined in `motif/lib/plant.mtf`.

### centromere

**`plant-centromere-178`**

Conserved segment of the 178 bp Arabidopsis centromeric repeat.

- pattern: `(fuzzy 3 "AGTATAAGAACTTAAACCGCAACCGATCTTAAAAGCCTAAGTAGTG")`

### circadian

**`ca1-binding-site`**

CCA1 binding site, the morning element of the plant circadian clock.

- pattern: `"AAAAATCT"`

**`circadian-cab`** *(template)*

Conserved element of chlorophyll a/b binding protein promoters.

- pattern: `"CAANNNNATC"`

**`evening-element`**

Evening element, the circadian clock output motif bound by CCA1 and LHY. Confers evening-
phased expression.

- pattern: `"AAAATATCT"`
- example: `AAAATATCT`
- reference: Harmer et al. 2000, Science 290:2110

### defence

**`tc-rich-repeat`**

TC-rich repeat involved in defence and stress responsiveness.

- pattern: `"ATTTTCTTCA"`

**`w-box`** *(template)*

W-box, bound by WRKY transcription factors. The invariant TTGAC core drives most plant
defence gene expression.

- pattern: `(iupac "TTGACY")`
- example: `TTGACC`
- reference: Eulgem et al. 2000, Trends Plant Sci 5:199

**`w-box-cluster`**

Two W-boxes within 60 bases, the arrangement typical of strongly pathogen-inducible
promoters.

- pattern: `(seq (named 'w1 (iupac "TTGACY")) (gap 5 60) (named 'w2 (iupac "TTGACY")))`

### hormone

**`abre`**

Abscisic acid responsive element. The ACGT core with a G at -2 is bound by bZIP AREB/ABF
factors; two copies are needed for a working ABA response.

- pattern: `(iupac "YACGTGGC")`
- example: `CACGTGGC`
- reference: Guiltinan et al. 1990, Science 250:267

**`abre-core`** *(template)*

The minimal ABA response element core ACGTG.

- pattern: `"ACGTG"`

**`auxre`**

Auxin response element, bound by ARF factors. TGTCTC is the classic form; the TGTC core is
the part that matters.

- pattern: `"TGTCTC"`
- example: `TGTCTC`
- reference: Ulmasov et al. 1997, Plant Cell 9:1963

**`auxre-core`** *(template)*

The minimal auxin response element core TGTCNN, present in DR5 reporters.

- pattern: `(iupac "TGTCNN")`

**`cgtca-motif`** *(template)*

CGTCA motif, the reverse orientation of the TGACG jasmonate element.

- pattern: `"CGTCA"`

**`erelee4`**

Ethylene responsive element from the tomato E4 gene.

- pattern: `(iupac "AWTTCAAA")`

**`gare`**

Gibberellin response element (GARE), bound by GAMYB in cereal aleurone.

- pattern: `(iupac "TAACARA")`
- example: `TAACAAA`

**`gcc-box`**

GCC box, the ethylene response element bound by ERF/AP2 factors. Central to pathogen-induced
PR gene expression.

- pattern: `"GCCGCC"`
- example: `AGCCGCC`
- reference: Ohme-Takagi & Shinshi 1995, Plant Cell 7:173

**`p-box`**

P-box, a gibberellin responsive element of cereal seed promoters.

- pattern: `"CCTTTTG"`

**`tatc-box`**

TATC box, a gibberellin responsive element.

- pattern: `"TATCCCA"`

**`tgacg-motif`** *(template)*

TGACG motif, responsive to methyl jasmonate and salicylic acid, bound by TGA bZIP factors.

- pattern: `"TGACG"`
- example: `TGACG`

### light

**`box-4`** *(template)*

Box 4, part of a conserved module in light-responsive plant promoters.

- pattern: `"ATTAAT"`

**`g-box`**

G-box CACGTG, the most widespread plant light-responsive element. Bound by bZIP factors such
as HY5 and by bHLH factors such as PIFs.

- pattern: `"CACGTG"`
- example: `CACGTG`
- reference: Giuliano et al. 1988, PNAS 85:7089

**`g-box-extended`**

The extended G-box context CCACGTGG.

- pattern: `"CCACGTGG"`

**`gata-motif-plant`** *(template)*

GATA motif, a light-responsive element bound by plant GATA factors.

- pattern: `(iupac "WGATAR")`

**`gt1-motif`** *(template)*

GT1 motif, a light-responsive element of the rbcS and other photosynthesis genes.

- pattern: `(iupac "GRWAAW")`

**`i-box`**

I-box, a conserved light-responsive element upstream of many photosynthesis-associated
nuclear genes.

- pattern: `"GATAAG"`
- example: `GATAAG`

**`rbcs-conserved`**

Conserved element upstream of rbcS genes encoding the Rubisco small subunit.

- pattern: `(fuzzy 1 "CCACGTGGCC")`

**`sp1-plant`** *(template)*

Light-responsive GC-rich element found in monocot promoters.

- pattern: `"GGGCGG"`

### meristem

**`cat-box`** *(template)*

CAT box, associated with meristem expression.

- pattern: `"GCCACT"`

### organelle

**`chloroplast-promoter`**

Plastid-encoded polymerase promoter. Chloroplasts kept the bacterial -35/-10 arrangement
from their cyanobacterial ancestor.

- pattern: `(seq (fuzzy 1 (iupac "TTGACA")) (gap 15 19) (fuzzy 1 (iupac "TATAAT")))`

### pollen

**`pollen-lat52`** *(template)*

POLLEN1LELAT52, a pollen-specific element from the tomato LAT52 promoter.

- pattern: `"AGAAA"`

### polyadenylation

**`plant-poly-a-signal`** *(template)*

Plant near-upstream element. Plants use AATAAA less strictly than animals; the surrounding
region is simply A-rich.

- pattern: `(alt "AATAAA" "AATAAT" "AATGAA")`

### promoter

**`as1-element`**

activation sequence-1 of the 35S promoter: two TGACG motifs four bases apart. Responds to
salicylic acid and auxin through TGA factors.

- pattern: `(seq "TGACG" (gap 4 4) "TGACG")`
- example: `TGACGtataTGACG`
- reference: Lam et al. 1989, PNAS 86:7890

**`camv-35s-tata`**

TATA box of the cauliflower mosaic virus 35S promoter, the constitutive promoter behind most
plant transgenes.

- pattern: `"TATATAA"`
- example: `TATATAA`

**`ccaat-box-plant`** *(template)*

CCAAT box bound by plant NF-Y (HAP) complexes.

- pattern: `"CCAAT"`

**`tata-box-plant`** *(template)*

Plant TATA box. Sits closer to the start site than in animals, typically at -25 to -30.

- pattern: `(iupac "TATAWAW")`

### seed

**`prolamin-box`** *(template)*

Prolamin box (endosperm box), driving cereal seed storage protein genes.

- pattern: `"TGTAAAG"`

**`ry-element`** *(template)*

RY element (Sph box), the seed-specific element bound by B3 domain factors such as ABI3 and
FUS3.

- pattern: `"CATGCA"`
- example: `CATGCATG`

**`skn-1-motif`** *(template)*

Skn-1 motif, required for endosperm expression.

- pattern: `"GTCAT"`

### splicing

**`plant-intron-donor`**

Plant 5' splice site. Plant introns are markedly U-rich, and that composition matters more
than the consensus itself.

- pattern: `(iupac "AGGTAAGT")`

### stress

**`crt-core`** *(template)*

The bare C-repeat / DRE core CCGAC.

- pattern: `"CCGAC"`

**`dre-crt`**

Dehydration responsive element / C-repeat. Bound by CBF/DREB1 in cold and DREB2 in drought.
The CCGAC core is the required part.

- pattern: `(iupac "RCCGAC")`
- example: `ACCGAC`
- reference: Yamaguchi-Shinozaki & Shinozaki 1994, Plant Cell 6:251

**`hse-plant`**

Plant heat shock element, inverted nGAAn repeats bound by plant HSFs.

- pattern: `(iupac "AGAANNTTCT")`
- example: `AGAACGTTCT`

**`ltre`**

Low temperature responsive element.

- pattern: `"CCGAAA"`

**`mbs`** *(template)*

MYB binding site involved in drought inducibility.

- pattern: `(iupac "CAACTG")`

### telomere

**`plant-telomere`**

Arabidopsis-type telomere repeat TTTAGGG.

- pattern: `"TTTAGGG"`

### tfbs

**`myb-core`** *(template)*

MYB recognition core, the general binding site of R2R3-MYB factors.

- pattern: `(iupac "CNGTTR")`
- example: `CAGTTA`

**`myc-core`** *(template)*

MYC recognition element (a plant E-box), bound by bHLH factors in ABA and jasmonate
signalling.

- pattern: `(iupac "CANNTG")`

### translation

**`plant-kozak`** *(template)*

Plant translation initiation context. Plants prefer A at -3 and A at +4, unlike the G that
vertebrates prefer at +4.

- pattern: `(iupac "AAMAATGGC")`
- example: `AACAATGGC`


## RNA elements

Defined in `motif/lib/rna.mtf`.

### ires

**`ires-poly-u`** *(template)*

Polypyrimidine tract of a type II internal ribosome entry site, found in picornaviral 5'
UTRs.

- pattern: `(seq (run "CU" 8 20) (gap 15 25) "AUG")`

### localisation

**`zipcode-beta-actin`** *(template)*

Beta-actin zipcode, the 54-base 3' UTR element that localises the message to the leading
edge of migrating fibroblasts. Core ACACCC.

- pattern: `"ACACCC"`

### mirna

**`mirna-seed-let7`**

Target site for the let-7 seed. let-7 was the second microRNA found and is conserved from
worms to humans.

- pattern: `"CUACCUC"`
- example: `CUACCUC`

**`mirna-seed-mir155`**

Target site for the miR-155 seed.

- pattern: `"AGCAUUAA"`

**`mirna-seed-mir21`**

Target site for the miR-21 seed. miR-21 is the microRNA most consistently over-expressed in
solid tumours.

- pattern: `"AUAAGCUA"`

**`pre-mirna-hairpin`** *(template)*

Precursor microRNA hairpin: a stem of roughly 22 base pairs with a terminal loop. Drosha and
Dicer cut it into the mature duplex.

- pattern: `(hairpin (stem 18 30) (loop 4 15) :wobble #t :mismatches 4)`

### modification

**`m6a-drach`** *(template)*

DRACH consensus of N6-methyladenosine. The methylated A is the fourth base. The commonest
instance is GGACU.

- pattern: `(iupac "DRACH")`
- example: `GGACU`
- reference: Dominissini et al. 2012, Nature 485:201

**`m6a-ggact`**

The GGACU core, the most frequently methylated DRACH instance.

- pattern: `"GGACU"`

**`pseudouridine-box-h`** *(template)*

H box of H/ACA snoRNAs, ANANNA, which guides pseudouridylation.

- pattern: `(iupac "ANANNA")`

**`snorna-c-box`** *(template)*

C box of C/D box snoRNAs, which guide 2'-O-methylation.

- pattern: `(iupac "RUGAUGA")`
- example: `AUGAUGA`

**`snorna-d-box`** *(template)*

D box of C/D box snoRNAs. The methylated nucleotide of the target sits exactly five bases
upstream of it.

- pattern: `"CUGA"`

### polyadenylation

**`poly-a-signal-rna`** *(template)*

Poly(A) signal AAUAAA in RNA form.

- pattern: `"AAUAAA"`

**`poly-a-tail`** *(template)*

A poly(A) tail: a run of at least 12 adenosines.

- pattern: `(run "A" 12)`

### recoding

**`slippery-site`** *(template)*

Ribosomal frameshift slippery heptamer X XXY YYZ. Followed by a downstream pseudoknot it
makes the ribosome shift back one base, as in retroviral gag-pol and coronavirus ORF1ab.

- pattern: `(iupac "NNNWWWH")`
- example: `UUUAAAC`
- reference: Brierley et al. 1989, Cell 57:537

**`slippery-site-canonical`**

The two most common slippery heptamers, UUUAAAC and UUUUUUA.

- pattern: `(alt "UUUAAAC" "UUUUUUA" "AAAAAAC" "GGGAAAC")`

### riboswitch

**`riboswitch-purine`** *(template)*

Conserved core of the purine riboswitch aptamer. The pyrimidine at position 74 decides
whether it reads guanine or adenine.

- pattern: `(seq (iupac "CUCUUAUA") (gap 20 60) (iupac "UAUAAUCG"))`

**`riboswitch-sam`** *(template)*

Conserved element of the S-adenosylmethionine (SAM-I) riboswitch, which controls methionine
and cysteine biosynthesis in Gram positives.

- pattern: `(seq (iupac "CUUAUCAAGAG") (gap 20 80) (iupac "GGCUGGUGG"))`

### rna-structure

**`kissing-loop-stem`** *(template)*

A stem-loop with a 6 to 8 base loop, the size that can form a kissing interaction with a
complementary loop.

- pattern: `(hairpin (stem 5 12) (loop 6 8) :wobble #t)`

**`rna-g-quadruplex`** *(template)*

RNA G-quadruplex. Unlike DNA quadruplexes these are almost always parallel, and they
regulate translation from 5' UTRs.

- pattern: `(seq (run "G" 2 5) (gap 1 7) (run "G" 2 5) (gap 1 7) (run "G" 2 5) (gap 1 7) (run "G" 2 5))`

**`rna-hairpin`** *(template)*

A generic RNA hairpin: a stem of 4 to 12 paired bases and a loop of 3 to 10. Wobble G:U
pairs are allowed, as they are in real RNA.

- pattern: `(hairpin (stem 4 12) (loop 3 10) :wobble #t)`
- example: `GGCGCuuuuGCGCC`

**`tetraloop-gnra`**

GNRA tetraloop, the most common stable loop in structured RNA. Closed by a strong stem, it
is unusually resistant to unfolding.

- pattern: `(hairpin (stem 4 10) (iupac "GNRA") :wobble #t)`
- example: `GGCCgcaaGGCC`
- reference: Woese et al. 1990, PNAS 87:8467

**`tetraloop-uncg`**

UNCG tetraloop, the other exceptionally stable tetraloop family.

- pattern: `(hairpin (stem 4 10) (iupac "UNCG") :wobble #t)`

### small-rna

**`piwi-1u`** *(template)*

The 5' uridine preference of piRNAs, a strong signature of the PIWI-bound small RNA class.

- pattern: `(seq at-start "U")`

### splicing-rna

**`branch-point-rna`** *(template)*

Branch point adenosine in its RNA context.

- pattern: `(iupac "YURAY")`
- example: `CUGAC`

**`u1-binding-site`** *(template)*

5' splice site as U1 snRNA sees it, in RNA form.

- pattern: `(iupac "MAGGURAGU")`

### stability

**`are-class-ii`**

Class II AU-rich element: overlapping AUUUA pentamers in a U-rich stretch, the strongly
destabilising form found in TNF and GM-CSF messages.

- pattern: `(seq (iupac "WW") "A" (repeat "UUUA" 2 6) (iupac "WW"))`
- example: `UUAUUUAUUUAUU`

**`are-nonamer`** *(template)*

The nonamer UUAUUUAWW used to score functional AU-rich elements.

- pattern: `(iupac "UUAUUUAWW")`

**`au-rich-element`**

AU-rich element core AUUUA. In a U-rich context it recruits decay factors and shortens the
life of cytokine and proto-oncogene messages.

- pattern: `"AUUUA"`
- example: `AUUUA`
- reference: Shaw & Kamen 1986, Cell 46:659

**`histone-stem-loop`**

Histone mRNA 3' stem-loop. Replication-dependent histone messages end here instead of
carrying a poly(A) tail; SLBP binds it.

- pattern: `(iupac "GGYYCUUUUMAGRGCC")`
- example: `GGCUCUUUUCAGAGCC`
- reference: Marzluff et al. 2008, Nat Rev Genet 9:843

**`pumilio-response-element`**

Pumilio response element UGUANAUA, bound by PUF proteins to repress and destabilise target
messages.

- pattern: `(iupac "UGUANAUA")`
- example: `UGUAAAUA`

### translation

**`cpe`** *(template)*

Cytoplasmic polyadenylation element, bound by CPEB to extend the poly(A) tail and activate
translation of stored maternal messages.

- pattern: `(iupac "UUUUUAU")`
- example: `UUUUUAU`

**`kozak-rna`** *(template)*

Kozak context in RNA form.

- pattern: `(iupac "GCCRCCAUGG")`

**`shine-dalgarno-rna`** *(template)*

Shine-Dalgarno sequence in RNA form, pairing with the 16S rRNA 3' end.

- pattern: `(iupac "AGGAGG")`

### trna

**`trna-acceptor-stem`** *(template)*

tRNA acceptor stem: seven base pairs at the ends of the molecule.

- pattern: `(hairpin (stem 7 7) (loop 50 80) :wobble #t :mismatches 1)`

**`trna-cca-end`** *(template)*

The CCA end added to every tRNA after transcription. The amino acid attaches to its terminal
adenosine.

- pattern: `(seq "CCA" at-end)`

**`trna-d-loop`** *(template)*

The dihydrouridine loop signature, a GG dinucleotide in a short loop.

- pattern: `(seq "GG" (gap 1 3) (any-of "AU"))`

**`trna-t-loop`** *(template)*

The T-loop signature GUUCGA (T-psi-C-G in the modified RNA). One of the most conserved
sequences in all of biology.

- pattern: `"GUUCGA"`
- example: `GUUCGA`

### utr

**`ire-loop`** *(template)*

The bare IRE apical loop CAGUGH, without the stem context.

- pattern: `(iupac "CAGUGH")`
- example: `CAGUGU`

**`iron-responsive-element`**

Iron responsive element. IRP1 and IRP2 bind the CAGUGH loop of this hairpin when iron is
scarce, blocking ferritin translation and stabilising the transferrin receptor message.

- pattern: `(hairpin (stem 5 6) (iupac "CAGUGH") :wobble #t)`
- example: `GGGGCCAGUGUGCCCC`
- reference: Hentze & Kuhn 1996, PNAS 93:8175

**`secis-core`** *(template)*

Core of the SECIS element, which recodes an in-frame UGA as selenocysteine. The non-Watson-
Crick quartet is the invariant part.

- pattern: `(seq (iupac "AUGA") (gap 8 20) (iupac "GA"))`
- reference: Walczak et al. 1996, RNA 2:367

**`upstream-orf`** *(template)*

An upstream open reading frame in a 5' UTR: a start codon in good context followed by a
short reading frame and a stop. uORFs dampen translation of the main ORF and mediate stress
responses.

- pattern: `(seq (any-of "AG") (gap 2 2) "AUG" (repeat (gap 3 3) 2 60) (alt "UAA" "UAG" "UGA"))`

### viral

**`rev-response-element-core`**

Stem-loop IIB core of the HIV-1 Rev response element, the site Rev binds to export unspliced
viral RNA.

- pattern: `(fuzzy 2 "GGGCGCAGCGUCAAUGACGCUGACGGUACAGGCC")`

**`tar-element`** *(template)*

HIV-1 TAR element apical loop, bound by Tat with cyclin T1 to allow transcription
elongation.

- pattern: `(fuzzy 1 "CUGGGA")`


## Protein motifs

Defined in `motif/lib/protein.mtf`.

### binding-site

**`fourteen-three-three-mode1`** *(template)*

Mode 1 binding motif for 14-3-3 proteins, recognised once the central serine is
phosphorylated.

- pattern: `(prosite "R-S-x-S-x-P")`

**`pdz-binding-c-terminal`** *(template)*

Class I PDZ-binding motif at the extreme C-terminus, which docks a protein into a scaffold
such as PSD-95.

- pattern: `(prosite "[ST]-x-[VIL]>")`
- example: `TKV`

**`rgd-motif`** *(template)*

RGD cell attachment sequence, the integrin binding tripeptide of fibronectin and many other
matrix proteins.

- pattern: `(prosite "R-G-D")`
- example: `RGD`
- reference: Pierschbacher & Ruoslahti 1984, Nature 309:30

**`sh2-binding`** *(template)*

Phosphotyrosine motif of an SH2 binding site.

- pattern: `(prosite "Y-x(2)-[ILVM]")`

**`sh3-binding-pxxp`** *(template)*

PxxP core of an SH3 binding site, a left-handed polyproline II helix.

- pattern: `(prosite "P-x(2)-P")`
- example: `PPLP`

### catalytic

**`aspartyl-protease`**

The D-T/S-G triad of aspartyl proteases such as pepsin, renin and HIV-1 protease.

- pattern: `(prosite "[LIVMFGAC]-[LIVMTADN]-[LIVFSA]-D-[ST]-G-[STAV]")`
- reference: PROSITE PS00141

**`atp-binding-abc`** *(template)*

ABC transporter signature, the LSGGQ motif that couples ATP hydrolysis to transport.

- pattern: `(prosite "L-S-G-G-[QE]")`
- example: `LSGGQ`

**`cysteine-protease`** *(template)*

The catalytic cysteine of the papain family in its conserved setting.

- pattern: `(prosite "Q-x(3)-[GE]-x-C-[YW]-x(2)-[STAGC]")`
- reference: PROSITE PS00139

**`glycine-rich-loop`** *(template)*

Glycine-rich (P-loop-like) nucleotide binding loop of protein kinases, GxGxxG. Not the same
as the Walker A P-loop, though it does the same job.

- pattern: `(prosite "G-x-G-x(2)-[GSA]")`
- example: `GEGSFG`

**`kinase-active-site`**

Serine/threonine kinase active site signature, built around the catalytic aspartate of the
HRD motif.

- pattern: `(prosite "[LIVMFYC]-x-[HY]-x-D-[LIVMFY]-K-x(2)-N-[LIVMFYCT](3)")`
- reference: PROSITE PS00108

**`kinase-atp-binding`**

Protein kinase ATP-binding signature: the glycine-rich loop of subdomain I followed by the
invariant VAIK lysine of subdomain II, which positions the alpha and beta phosphates of ATP.

- pattern: `(prosite "[LIV]-G-{P}-G-{P}-[FYWMGSTNH]-[SGA]-{PW}-[LIVCAT]-{PD}-x-[GSTACLIVMFY]-x(5,18)-[LIVMFYWCSTAR]-[AIVP]-[LIVMFAGCKR]-K")`
- reference: PROSITE PS00107

**`kinase-dfg`** *(template)*

DFG motif at the start of the kinase activation loop. Its in and out conformations decide
whether type I or type II inhibitors bind.

- pattern: `(prosite "D-F-G")`
- example: `DFG`

**`kinase-hrd`** *(template)*

HRD motif of the protein kinase catalytic loop, whose aspartate is the catalytic base.

- pattern: `(prosite "H-[RC]-D-[LIVMFY]-[KR]-x(2)-N")`

**`lipase-gxsxg`**

GxSxG nucleophile elbow of the alpha/beta hydrolase fold, present in lipases, esterases and
serine hydrolases generally.

- pattern: `(prosite "[LIV]-x-[LIVFY]-[LIVMST]-G-[HYWV]-S-x-G-[GSTAC]")`
- reference: PROSITE PS00120

**`serine-protease-catalytic`**

GDSGGP signature around the catalytic serine of the chymotrypsin family.

- pattern: `(prosite "G-D-S-G-G-P")`
- example: `GDSGGP`

**`serine-protease-histidine`** *(template)*

The histidine of the chymotrypsin family catalytic triad in its conserved context.

- pattern: `(prosite "[LIVM]-[ST]-A-[STAG]-H-C")`
- reference: PROSITE PS00134

**`thioredoxin-active-site`** *(template)*

CXXC redox active site of thioredoxins and the wider thioredoxin fold.

- pattern: `(prosite "W-C-[GATP]-[PLIVMFYWC]-C")`
- example: `WCGPC`

**`tyrosine-kinase-active-site`**

Tyrosine kinase active site signature.

- pattern: `(prosite "[LIVMFYC]-x-[HY]-x-D-[LIVMFY]-[RSTAC]-x(2)-N-[LIVMFYC](3)")`
- reference: PROSITE PS00109

**`zinc-metalloprotease`**

HExxH zinc binding motif of the metzincin and gluzincin metalloproteases. Two histidines and
a downstream glutamate hold the catalytic zinc.

- pattern: `(prosite "H-E-x(2)-H")`
- example: `HELGH`
- reference: PROSITE PS00142

### degradation

**`d-box`** *(template)*

Destruction box, the signal that marks mitotic cyclins for the anaphase promoting complex.

- pattern: `(prosite "R-x(2)-L-x(4)-N")`
- example: `RAALGVIQN`

**`ken-box`** *(template)*

KEN box, the second APC/C degradation signal, read by Cdh1.

- pattern: `(prosite "K-E-N")`
- example: `KEN`

**`pest-region`**

A proline, glutamate, serine and threonine rich stretch flanked by basic residues,
associated with rapid proteasomal turnover.

- pattern: `(seq (any-of "KRH") (run "PESTDNQGAV" 12 40) (any-of "KRH"))`
- reference: Rogers et al. 1986, Science 234:364

### domain

**`bzip-basic-region`** *(template)*

Basic region of a bZIP factor, immediately upstream of the zipper, with the invariant
N-x(7)-R spacing that contacts DNA.

- pattern: `(prosite "N-x(7)-R-x(2)-[LIVM]")`

**`ef-hand`**

EF-hand calcium binding loop. The aspartate at position 1 and the glutamate at position 12
grip the calcium ion.

- pattern: `(prosite "D-x-[DNS]-{ILVFYW}-[DENSTG]-[DNQGHRK]-{GP}-[LIVMC]-[DENQSTAGC]-x(2)-[DE]-[LIVMFYW]")`
- reference: PROSITE PS00018

**`ldl-receptor-class-b`** *(template)*

YWTD repeat signature of the LDL receptor class B propeller.

- pattern: `(prosite "Y-W-[ST]-D")`

**`leucine-zipper`**

Leucine zipper: leucines every seventh residue over four heptads, the dimerisation element
of bZIP transcription factors.

- pattern: `(prosite "L-x(6)-L-x(6)-L-x(6)-L")`
- reference: Landschulz et al. 1988, Science 240:1759

**`p-loop`**

P-loop, the Walker A motif that cradles the phosphates of bound ATP or GTP. The most common
nucleotide binding element in all of biology.

- pattern: `(prosite "[AG]-x(4)-G-K-[ST]")`
- example: `GPSSSGKT`

### Methylation and modification sites

**`cpg-dinucleotide`** &mdash; The CpG dinucleotide, the target of vertebrate DNA methylation and the reason HpaII (blocked by methylation) and MspI (not blocked) give different digests.

**`dam-site`** &mdash; Dam methyltransferase target GATC in E. coli; the A is N6-methylated. DpnI needs it methylated, MboI needs it unmethylated, DpnII is blocked by it.

**`dcm-site`** &mdash; Dcm methyltransferase target CCWGG; the inner C is C5-methylated. Blocks some enzymes and is the reason plasmids from dcm+ strains resist certain digests.

**`ecoki-site`** &mdash; EcoKI type I restriction/modification recognition site of E. coli K-12.
